IVORY ROYAL MEDALLION TL 13131

A Lippan art mural in three vitrified tiles. Rings of mirror-effect dots, a scalloped arch and paisley scrolls bring the clay-and-mirror relief of Kutch to a wall you can wipe clean.

Hand-built Lippan is made on site in clay and glass, and needs to stay dry. This tile carries the pattern on a dense vitrified body, so diya soot and ghee wipe off with a damp cloth.

Installation guide
  1. Check the wall is flat, dry and plastered.
  2. Lay all three tiles on the floor in order and check that the mandala lines up.
  3. Fix with a tile adhesive made for large-format wall tiles, starting with the bottom tile.
  4. Grout and wipe the face clean before the grout sets.
Care and maintenance

Dust with a dry cloth. Wipe marks, diya soot and ghee with a soft, damp cloth and mild soap, then dry. Avoid acid cleaners and scouring pads.

Designer’s take

Treat it as the one feature wall in the room and keep the walls beside it plain. Light it from above or the side so the pattern reads.

Material pairings

Warm wood, brass and a plain stone or marble-look floor sit well with the ivory and charcoal of the mural.
